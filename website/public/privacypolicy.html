1. Who we are, and which hat we wear

Botlien is an early-stage company building financial telemetry for robot fleet operators. We turn the operating data a robot already reports into what its work is worth against what it costs to lease.

We handle two different kinds of data, and the law treats them differently, so we keep them apart throughout this policy:

  • Website visitors and prospects. Here we are the controller. We decide what is collected and why, and sections 2 to 4 describe it.
  • Customer fleet data. Here we are a processor. The customer is the controller, we act on their documented instructions, and section 5 describes it. If you are an employee or contractor of one of our customers and you have a question about that data, your employer is the right place to start, and we will support them in answering you.

You can reach us any time at info@botlien.com.

2. What we collect on this website

We run our own lightweight, first-party analytics so we can tell whether anyone is reading this site. It is deliberately minimal. For each visit we record:

  • A random session identifier, generated in your browser and held in sessionStorage. It is not a cookie, it contains nothing about you, and it disappears when you close the tab.
  • The page path you viewed and roughly how many seconds you spent on it.
  • The referring website's hostname, if you arrived from another site. We store the hostname only, never the full URL.
  • A two-letter country code, derived by our hosting provider from the network connection.

We do not store your IP address. We do not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, cross-site trackers, session recording, or heatmaps. We do not build a profile of you, and we cannot identify you from this data.

3. Cookies

This site sets one cookie, and only for our own staff. Signing in to our internal analytics dashboard at /stats sets botlien_stats_auth, which holds a hashed session token, is HttpOnly and Secure, and expires after thirty days. It is strictly necessary for that sign-in to work and it is never set for ordinary visitors.

There are no other cookies on this site: no advertising, analytics, or third-party cookies of any kind. We mention the one that exists because a policy that claims none at all would be inaccurate.

4. When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, company name and work email, and optionally a phone number and a message. When you submit it, that information is sent to us as an ordinary email and answered by a person. We use it to reply to you and to keep track of conversations we are having.

We also record that a submission happened, together with the company name, so we can count how many enquiries the site produces.

If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence for the same reason. We do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for, and we do not sell or rent contact details to anyone.

5. Customer fleet data

If your company becomes a Botlien customer, we process operating data about your robots as your processor. This is the core of the product, so it is worth being exact about it.

What we read

Robot telemetry and the commercial terms you give us: task and mission counts, uptime and availability, cycle times, error and fault records, battery and consumable wear, and the lease or financing terms for each unit. You either upload an export or authorise access through your robot vendor's own credential system.

Where it comes from

Either directly from you, or from your robot manufacturer's API under access you have granted and can withdraw. Access is governed by a signed data access agreement that you control.

What we do with it

We normalise it across brands, price the work against the cost of the same work done by a person, compare that to what the units cost, and surface the results to you. We use it to run the product for you and to keep the product working. We act on your documented instructions and do not use it for our own purposes.

A data processing agreement

Where you need one, including under Article 28 of the GDPR, we will sign a data processing agreement. Ask us at info@botlien.com.

6. Data about your workforce

Robot telemetry is machine data. It is not intended to describe identifiable people, and we do not ask for anything that does.

We are explicit about this because the risk is real rather than theoretical. A fleet export that ties a robot to a shift, a station, or a named operator can turn a machine-performance tool into a record about a worker. So:

  • Do not send us biometric data. We do not accept it and we have no use for it.
  • If your export identifies workers, tell us. If you provide data that identifies individual employees or contractors, you confirm you have given them whatever notice, and obtained whatever consent or consultation, your local law requires. In parts of Europe that includes works-council consultation.
  • Where such data does reach us, we process it only to deliver the service to you, on your instructions, and we will help you respond to any request one of your people makes about it.

Botlien is built to answer whether a robot is paying for itself. It is not designed, sold, or intended as a tool for monitoring or scoring individual workers.

7. What we never do

  • We never connect to your bank, and we never read bank statements, accounting systems, or financial statements.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it for anyone else's advertising.
  • We never show one customer's fleet data to another customer.
  • We may use aggregated, anonymised statistics that cannot identify you or your fleet to improve the product. We will ask your permission before publishing anything derived from your fleet, and before naming you anywhere public.

9. Where the data lives

Our infrastructure is operated in the United States. If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, or Switzerland, that means your data is transferred outside your home jurisdiction.

Where such a transfer requires a safeguard, we will put the UK or EU Standard Contractual Clauses in place as part of a data processing agreement before processing begins. If you need that in writing before signing anything, ask and we will send it.

11. Your rights, and our legal basis

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive a copy, or to object to how we use it. If you are in California, that includes rights under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA. If you are in the EEA or UK, that includes rights under the GDPR.

Where the GDPR applies and we act as controller, we rely on our legitimate interests in understanding whether our website works and in responding to people who contact us about our product. Those interests are modest and the data is minimal, which is what keeps the balance in your favour. Where we act as processor for a customer, the legal basis is the customer's, not ours.

We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about individuals that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise any of these, email info@botlien.com. We will respond within the time the applicable law requires, and we will not treat you differently for asking. If we process fleet data on behalf of a customer, requests about it should go through them, and we will support them in answering.

12. Security and breach notification

Access to customer data is limited to the people who need it to run the service. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Credentials for vendor APIs are stored as secrets, never in the codebase. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise, but keeping the data we hold narrow is itself a security decision: we do not hold bank credentials or financial statements, so they cannot be lost.

If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected customers without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, with what we know at the time, and we will keep updating you as we learn more. We will not wait until we have the full picture to tell you something happened.

13. Children

This is a business product. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 25, 2026

Botlien reads robot telemetry, not bank data. This page explains what we collect when you visit this site or talk to us, what we do with a customer's fleet data, and what we will never do with either.

The short version. This website sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party trackers. If you fill in the contact form, that message is emailed to us and answered by a person. If you become a customer, we read only the robot telemetry your data access agreement covers, we never connect to your bank, and we never sell or share your data.

1. Who we are

Botlien is an early-stage company building financial telemetry for robot fleet operators. We turn the operating data a robot already reports into what its work is worth against what it costs to lease. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

You can reach us any time at info@botlien.com.

2. What we collect on this website

We run our own lightweight, first-party analytics so we can tell whether anyone is reading this site. It is deliberately minimal. For each visit we record:

  • A random session identifier, generated in your browser and held in sessionStorage. It is not a cookie, it contains nothing about you, and it disappears when you close the tab.
  • The page path you viewed and roughly how many seconds you spent on it.
  • The referring website's hostname, if you arrived from another site. We store the hostname only, never the full URL.
  • A two-letter country code, derived by our hosting provider from the network connection.

We do not store your IP address. We do not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, cross-site trackers, session recording, or heatmaps. We do not build a profile of you, and we cannot identify you from this data.

3. When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, company name and work email, and optionally a phone number and a message. When you submit it, that information is sent to us as an ordinary email and answered by a person. We use it to reply to you and to keep track of conversations we are having.

We also record that a submission happened, together with the company name, so we can count how many enquiries the site produces.

If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence for the same reason. We do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for, and we do not sell or rent contact details to anyone.

4. Customer fleet data

If your company becomes a Botlien customer, we process operating data about your robots. This is the core of the product, so it is worth being exact about it.

What we read

Robot telemetry and the commercial terms you give us: task and mission counts, uptime and availability, cycle times, error and fault records, battery and consumable wear, and the lease or financing terms for each unit. You either upload an export or authorise access through your robot vendor's own credential system.

Where it comes from

Either directly from you, or from your robot manufacturer's API under access you have granted and can withdraw. Access is governed by a signed data access agreement that you control.

What we do with it

We normalise it across brands, price the work against the cost of the same work done by a person, compare that to what the units cost, and surface the results to you. We use it to run the product for you and to keep the product working.

Personal information inside fleet data

Robot telemetry is machine data. It is not intended to contain information about identifiable people, and we do not ask for any. Where a customer's export happens to include an operator name or a similar field, we process it only as part of delivering the service, and we act on your instructions with respect to it.

5. What we never do

  • We never connect to your bank, and we never read bank statements, accounting systems, or financial statements.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it for anyone else's advertising.
  • We never show one customer's fleet data to another customer.
  • We never use one customer's fleet data to build a product feature that identifies or exposes that customer, and we will ask first before using anonymised, aggregated figures in anything public.

7. How long we keep things

  • Website analytics: aggregate counts, kept while they remain useful. They contain no identifiers that point to a person.
  • Enquiries and correspondence: kept for as long as the conversation is live, and for a reasonable period afterwards so we remember who we have spoken to.
  • Customer fleet data: kept for the term of the agreement. When an agreement ends, we delete or return it as that agreement specifies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive a copy, or to object to how we use it. If you are in California, that includes rights under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA. If you are in the EEA or UK, that includes rights under the GDPR.

We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

To exercise any of these, email info@botlien.com. We will respond within the time the applicable law requires, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

If we process fleet data on behalf of a customer, we act on that customer's instructions, and requests about it should go through them.

9. Security

Access to customer data is limited to the people who need it to run the service. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Credentials for vendor APIs are stored as secrets, never in the codebase. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise, but keeping the data we hold narrow is itself a security decision: we do not hold bank credentials or financial statements, so they cannot be lost.

10. Children

This is a business product. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and move the date at the top. If a change is material and affects customers, we will tell them directly rather than relying on you to re-read this page.

12. How to reach us

Questions, requests, or anything that reads wrong here: info@botlien.com.

Robot telemetry only. Never bank data or financial statements.

3. What the service is

Botlien reads operating data your robots already report, prices the work they performed against what the same work costs when a person does it, compares that to what the units cost to lease or finance, and shows you the result. It flags units that are not covering their cost while there is still time to act.

Botlien is an analysis tool. It does not operate your robots, it does not move money, and it does not connect to your bank.

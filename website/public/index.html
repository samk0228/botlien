From robot telemetry to financial telemetry
Financial telemetry
Task completions, uptime, and utilization converted into what each robot's work is worth, valued at what a person doing the same work costs, against what the unit costs to lease.
Cash flow analysis & projections
Current-period actuals and a forward-looking view that factors in depreciation and financing costs alongside what each robot's work is worth. Botlien is the financial layer underneath your fleet, not a SaaS report bolted on top of it.
Shortfall, weeks early
A robot running under its lease surfaces while you can still act, move a unit, renegotiate, or hold the next lease, not after the month closes and the money has already moved.
Every brand, one number
Mixed fleets are normal, and every vendor reports differently. Botlien normalizes them onto one measure, so an arm from one manufacturer and an AMR from another are judged the same way, against what each costs you. Your vendor's own dashboard can only ever show you their half of the floor.
Why this is hard to copy
Financial, not operational
Fleet-management incumbents stop at uptime and fault codes. Turning that exhaust into coverage, cash flow, and what the work is worth is credit work, not dashboard work, and it is the layer nobody else is building.
Cross-brand normalization
No single manufacturer has a reason to build this, it would mean showing you how their robots compare to a competitor's failure rate. Working across brands is exactly what makes it valuable, and exactly what one OEM can't replicate.
Locks in as you grow
Once real telemetry history exists and a lender relationship is layered on top, that access becomes contractual, part of your own borrowing base or lease covenant, not something a competitor can copy overnight.
Where this goes next
A clean, verifiable performance history is, over time, the foundation for better financing terms: a proven fleet should cost less to finance than one nobody is watching. Analytics is how Botlien enters; helping price that flow is where it goes.

FAQ

Common questions.

Why the name Botlien?
It's "bot" plus "lien," the legal claim a lender holds on financed equipment. Botlien turns a financed robot fleet's own telemetry into the cash flow and credit risk picture that claim is actually based on.
What does Botlien actually do?
Botlien turns an operator's robot telemetry, task completions, uptime, utilization, into financial telemetry: coverage against the lease, cash flow, and early warning, built entirely from the fleet's own performance data.
Is my fleet's data safe with Botlien?
Botlien reads robot telemetry only, task and performance data already exposed by manufacturers like Pudu and Gausium. It never touches bank data or financial statements, and access is governed by a signed data agreement the operator controls.

## Future ideas & feature backlog

- ML-based risk scoring, once enough historical default/repossession data exists to train against (explicitly not v1 — rules-based only for now).
- Additional robot brand connectors beyond the first two (Phase 3 validates the normalization schema is reusable; more brands is the natural extension after that).
- Expansion beyond service robots into other financed-equipment categories with similar telemetry-access patterns, if the covenant-based data-access model proves out — not scoped or evaluated yet.
- **Added 2026-08-04, from a longer strategy session — real applications, not yet scoped or prioritized:**
  - Securitization diligence layer: once a RaaS operator's lease book is big enough to sell/securitize to institutional investors, Botlien could be the verification layer that makes that receivables pool actually sellable (institutional buyers won't buy a pool of leases on robots they can't verify are still functioning). Same shape as auto-lease securitization relying on VIN-level data.
  - Cross-brand benchmarking product, sold to lenders deciding which robot brands are safe to finance in the first place — a pre-loan underwriting input, not just post-loan monitoring. Deliberately held back from the first pilot (see OEM adverse-selection note in Build.md's Go-to-market sequencing) until there's enough brand coverage that it doesn't just expose one early OEM partner's weaknesses.
  - Insurance underwriting feed — same telemetry, different buyer (usage-based insurance for commercial robots).
  - Resale/secondary-market valuation — a robot with a clean telemetry history is worth more used, same logic as CARFAX for cars. Ties directly into the depreciation/condition-multiplier collateral-value work in Build.md's Risk scoring engine section.
  - Data licensed back to the OEM itself for predictive maintenance/warranty cost reduction — flips the customer relationship, and needs separate consent from what a lending covenant authorizes (don't reuse loan-covenant data for a second commercial purpose without checking this).

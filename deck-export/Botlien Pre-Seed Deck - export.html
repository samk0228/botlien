Your robots are working. Is your business?

Robot telemetry, turned into financial telemetry, cash flow, and default risk, weeks before it hits a bank statement.

Two different clocks

The robot's clock

Continuous. Every trip, fault, charge cycle and idle hour is logged the moment it happens.

The business's clock

Monthly, in arrears. The operator and the lender learn about a shortfall after the money has already moved.

Every hardware-financed category eventually grows an underwriting layer. Robotics is the last large category still without one.

Telemetry, translated into cash flow

01
Robot telemetry

Utilization, uptime, fault codes and location, pulled by API from the fleet the operator already runs.

02
Financial telemetry

Each unit's activity converted into a leading signal of financial health: falling utilization today is churn, an SLA credit, or a missed renewal tomorrow.

03
Cash flow analysis & projection

Fleet-level inflow and outflow, projected forward from live utilization rather than last month's close.

04
Default & credit risk

A shortfall surfaced weeks early, for the operator making payroll and the lender holding the paper.

Real hurdles, and what each one opens up

Is robot adoption growing fast enough to matter?
$16.7B

Global robot install value, an all-time high. RaaS growing ~18–21% CAGR, already accelerating toward ~32% under an elevated-adoption case (page 4).

→ Pulls the lender-expansion product forward sooner than modeled.

What if manufacturers never grant real access?
85%+

Vehicle telematics adoption once lenders required it, the exact mechanism this is built on. Phase 0 proves it first (page 10), before anything else is built.

→ One access relationship becomes the template for manufacturer partnerships.

Will operators actually pay for this?
~12%

Of financing payment, not a flat SaaS fee (page 5). One caught at-risk robot covers years of it at these prices.

→ Paying usage history is what makes the lender-data product credible later.

Can a two-person team execute this?
2 → 5

Founders today. Funded pace adds one below-market engineer and two paid interns (page 12), a staged build, not a stretch.

→ Same lean, staged model scales the team again at seed.
